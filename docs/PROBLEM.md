# Problem

Students and young professionals in Iloilo City often struggle to find a dorm that feels safe, affordable, and close to school, work, or transport hubs. Many listings are scattered across Facebook groups and messaging apps, with outdated photos, vague room details, and no easy way to compare nearby options.

This causes stress before school starts and makes it hard to make a decision quickly when a room is available.

## Person we are helping

A student named Althea is looking for a dorm near the city center. She wants:

- a place that is safe and within a reasonable budget
- clear room photos and monthly rental details
- nearby access to schools, markets, and public transport
- an easy way to compare different dorms in Iloilo City

## MVP cut

The app should help a renter browse verified-looking dorm listings in Iloilo City, compare room features and prices, and view each property on a map with a photo gallery. The first version is a browsing and discovery website, not a booking system.
