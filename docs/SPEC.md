# Spec: RentaDorm Iloilo

## Product goal

Create a public-facing website for renting dorms in Iloilo City. The site should make it easy for renters to browse multiple listings, compare room features, and view each dorm on a map.

## User

Primary user: a student or worker looking for a dorm in Iloilo City.

## Core flow

1. The user opens the homepage.
2. They scan featured dorm listings with photos, prices, and room details.
3. They filter or select dorms by area.
4. They view the selected dorm in a larger detail panel with amenities and a photo gallery.
5. They see where the dorm is located on a map pinned to its location.
6. They can decide which room is the best fit based on price, area, and convenience.

## Features

- Hero section with branding for RentaDorm Iloilo
- Featured dorm listings with image, location, rental price, amenities, and rating
- Area filter chips for common Iloilo districts
- Selected dorm detail panel showing room description, amenities, and map
- Embedded map that updates to the chosen dorm's location
- Photo gallery for the selected dorm
- Clean mobile-friendly layout optimized for browsing on a phone

## Non-goals

- No login or authentication
- No booking or reservation flow
- No payment processing
- No admin panel

## Success criteria

- The homepage demonstrates at least five dorm listings in Iloilo City
- The selected dorm changes the visible detail panel and map location
- The page is readable and visually appealing on desktop and mobile devices
- The experience is focused on quick dorm discovery, not full property management
