# RentaDorm Iloilo

RentaDorm Iloilo is a simple housing discovery website for students and workers who are searching for dorms in Iloilo City. Instead of chasing scattered posts on social media, renters can open one page and compare available rooms by location, price, amenities, and neighborhood.

The app shows featured dorms with photos, a quick summary of each room, and a map pin so users can see exactly where the property is located. This helps people move faster and makes the decision easier when they need a safe and convenient place to stay.

In the first version, we are focusing on browsing and comparison only. The product is fast, easy to understand, and built for a real local need in Iloilo City.
